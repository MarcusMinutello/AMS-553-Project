# AMS 553 / CSE 529: Final Projects

## Team Size

Teams may have **1 to 4 students** (no more than 4).

## Possible Project Topics

1. Generating correlated random variates and/or non-stationary stochastic processes.
2. Importance sampling techniques for estimating rare event probabilities.
3. Simulation-based optimization algorithms (e.g., stochastic approximation, simulated annealing, nested partitions method, and many others) applied to a specific problem of interest.
4. Studying and/or comparing the performance of two or more simulation-based optimization algorithms on a set of benchmark optimization problems.
5. Simulation-based approaches for stochastic dynamic programming.
6. Simulation of manufacturing systems (e.g., inventory, production).
7. Simulation of traffic systems (air, road, water, computer networks).
8. Simulation of many other real systems of interest.
9. Your own proposed topic, other than those suggested above.

## Timeline

| Milestone | Due Date |

## Deliverables

### 1. Proposal

- One page, covering the project team and a summary of the proposed topic.
- Only **one proposal per team**.
- Must describe:
  - The proposed project topic.
  - How the workload will be distributed among team members.

### 2. Presentation

- **Length:** 10 to 20 minutes. Keep it brief and try not to go over time.
- **Format:** In person, in the regular classroom. The detailed schedule will be provided later.
- **Presenters:** A single representative, or multiple team members presenting jointly.
- **Must include:**
  - A concise description of the project's objectives.
  - The main techniques and tools used (e.g., input/output analysis tools, variance reduction methods).
  - The expected outcomes and insights once the project is complete.

### 3. Final Written Report

- **Deadline:** December 9 at 5:00 PM. This is the absolute deadline; no extensions.
- **Length:** At least 4 pages, including the title, group member names, and all figures and tables, but **not** program code.
- **Structure:** Begin with a summary of the problems and results.
- **Format:** PDF preferred; MS Word is also accepted.
- **Submission:** Email it directly to the instructor at jiaqiao.hu.1@stonybrook.edu.
